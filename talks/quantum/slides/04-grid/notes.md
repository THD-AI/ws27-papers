# 4 · Only about half of a logical qubit's qubits store anything

In the surface code the qubits sit on a square grid, and its size is called the distance. The smallest grid, distance 3, has 9 storing qubits and 8 helpers: 17 in all. Distance 5 has 49, distance 7 has 97.

**Only about half of a logical qubit's qubits store anything.**
