# 1 · One recording from Google's chip gave two error rates

In December 2024 Google published the first quantum memory that gets more reliable as it gets bigger. One detail in that paper is easy to miss. The chip ran, and everything it reported was recorded.

Then two programs read the same recording. The standard one, matching, and a neural network, a program that learns from examples. The network's error rate came out about one in six lower. Same chip, same data. Only the reader changed.

That reader is a classical program, and in this course it is where machine learning works for quantum hardware. We asked what it has to do, why a network can do it better, and what that costs.
