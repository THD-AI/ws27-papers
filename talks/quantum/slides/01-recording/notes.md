# 1 · One recording from Google's chip gave two error rates

In December 2024 Google reported a surface-code memory that got more reliable at each of three grid sizes. In it, the chip ran and everything it reported was recorded.

Then two programs read the same recording. The standard one, matching, and a neural network, a program that learns from examples. At distance 7, the network's error rate came out about sixteen percent lower.

That reader is a classical program, and in this course it is where machine learning works for quantum hardware. We asked what it has to do, why a network can do it better, and what that costs.
