# 6 · One film frame shows what changed in that round

While the computer runs, the chip sends the check answers. We draw the changes from the round before as blue dots on a film. A round of checks takes 1.1 millionths of a second, about 900,000 rounds a second. At distance 7 there are 48 checks, so in one blink the chip puts out over four million results, for a single logical qubit.

A check can also report wrongly. Then it fires twice in a row, once wrong and once right again. So the rounds are read in sequence.
