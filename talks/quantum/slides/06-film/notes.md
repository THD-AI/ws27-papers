# 6 · The chip puts out a film of fired checks, one frame per round

While the computer runs, this is all that comes out. A round of checks takes 1.1 millionths of a second, about 900,000 rounds a second. At distance 7 there are 48 checks, so in one blink the chip puts out over four million results, for a single logical qubit.

A check can also report wrongly. Then it fires twice in a row, once wrong and once right again. So the rounds are read in sequence.

**The chip puts out a film of fired checks, one frame per round.**
