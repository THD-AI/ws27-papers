# 15 · A decoder gives no quantum speed-up, but its accuracy changes how big a useful machine must be

In the course's four properties. Performance: ours passed plain matching at distance 5, and correlated matching stayed about a fifth ahead. Speed-up: none, this is classical software serving the chip. Relevance: on Google's data the reader alone moved lambda from 2.04 to 2.14, and with lambda near 2 one more halving costs 64 qubits. Availability: correlated matching is free and needs no training, and a network that keeps pace live is so far only claimed.

By our own stop rule the idea survives: a laptop could train it in under two hours per seed. The second test, Google's real recordings, is still open.

**A decoder gives no quantum speed-up, but its accuracy changes how big a useful machine must be.**
