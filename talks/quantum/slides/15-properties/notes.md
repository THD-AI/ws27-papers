# 15 · A decoder gives no quantum speed-up, but its accuracy changes how big a useful machine must be

In the course's four properties. Performance: ours passed plain matching at distance 5, and it had about a fifth more failures than correlated matching. Speed-up: none, this is classical software serving the chip. Relevance: on Google's data the reader alone moved lambda from 2.04 to 2.14, and with lambda near 2 one more halving costs 64 qubits. Availability: correlated matching is free and needs no training.

Our stop rule had two tests. We trained on a GPU. The short timing runs suggest about an hour and three-quarters per seed on the laptop. We did not finish a long run on it. The test on Google's real recordings is still open.

**A decoder gives no quantum speed-up, but its accuracy changes how big a useful machine must be.**
