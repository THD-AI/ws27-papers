# 12 · The network needed 24 millionths of a second for a round the chip finishes in 1.1

The trouble is time. The chip finishes a round every 1.1 millionths of a second. Google's paper quotes 24 for its network, per round. By the time it has answered round 3, about 73 millionths in, the chip is at round 66, and 62 rounds are waiting. The gap keeps growing. So the network's results were worked out afterwards, on recordings.

The decoder that ran live was a fast, plain matching, on a smaller chip at distance 5, and it kept pace. A preprint from December 2025 claims a network that keeps pace too. Nobody has reviewed it yet.

**The network needed 24 millionths of a second for a round the chip finishes in 1.1.**
