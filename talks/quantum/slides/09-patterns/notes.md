# 9 · Matching gives the less likely answer on 24 of the 256 patterns we counted

We went down to the smallest grid, where every case can be counted, and compared two answers for every pattern of fired checks: matching's and the most likely one. On 24 of the 256 patterns, matching's answer is less likely than another. On 144 more, two answers are exactly as likely, and no decoder can do better there. The 24 are rare: together under one case in a hundred. The five percent error rate here only makes the counting easy; a real chip is far better.
