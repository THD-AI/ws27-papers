# 9 · We counted patterns and how often they occur

We went down to the smallest grid, where every case can be counted, and compared two answers for every pattern of fired checks: matching's and the most likely one. On 24 of the 256 patterns, matching's answer is less likely than another. On 144 more, two or four outcomes tie for best, and matching gives one of them. Those 24 patterns account for about 0.9 percent of rounds in this toy model. We gave each storing qubit a five percent chance of an error and assumed the checks never report wrongly.
