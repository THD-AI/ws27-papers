# 11 · On Google's recordings the network made about one error in six fewer

Back to Google. Each step from distance 3 to 5 to 7 cut the logical errors roughly in half. Google read the same recordings with two decoders. Its best matching decoder already links the two kinds of check in a limited way, and reached lambda 2.04: 171 errors per 100,000 rounds at distance 7. A neural network, trained on simulations and then on the chip's own data, reached lambda 2.14: 143. Learning decoders can also take in errors that plain matching's picture has no place for, like qubits that slip into a third state.

**On Google's recordings the network made about one error in six fewer.**
