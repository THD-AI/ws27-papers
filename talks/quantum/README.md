# The talk: The other half of a quantum computer

Quantum Computing seminar, TH Deggendorf, group talk, Saturday 9 January 2027, 15 minutes.

The talk is in [`v6/`](v6/README.md): eighteen pages and nine backups, built by `node tools/qtalk-site.mjs` into one file to present from and the talk's page on the site. [QUESTIONS.md](QUESTIONS.md) is the drill for the questions (its slide numbers are the first deck's).

Earlier decks are kept under tags, not in this folder: the first sixteen-slide deck and its redesign (`talks/quantum/index.html`, `slides/`, `kit.js`, `tools/qdeck.mjs`, `tools/qdeck-site.mjs`) under `archive/quantum-deck-16`; v3.1, v4 and the player under `archive/quantum-deck-v31`, `archive/quantum-deck-v4` and `archive/quantum-deck-player`.
