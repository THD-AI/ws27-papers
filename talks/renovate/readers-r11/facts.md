## Required fixes

1. **"Telekom says 40 percent" (p7 lands-on, p5 notes).** Telekom's own CEO slide shows "~32% Contacts handled, AI Agent"; only the press release says 40. Say "Telekom's press release says 40 percent", and if there is time add "even Telekom's investor slide shows 32".
2. **Quotation marks on p5–7 (“handled by AI agents”).** Not the release's words. Use the verbatim "now handle 40 percent of customer contacts", or drop the quotation marks.
3. **p22 notes: "on monitoring at work they are [more pessimistic than Americans]".** The Eurobarometer asked in the EU only, so there's nothing to compare with. Say "on monitoring at work they stand out even in the EU: second only to Sweden". Soften the B4 title the same way ("On monitoring: among the most negative in the EU").
4. **p8–9 source line "T-Mobile reports the fall as 55%".** T-Mobile's slide says ">55%"; the flat 55% is Telekom's release. Write "T-Mobile reports more than 55%".
5. **p20 lands-on "Both had to walk it back".** True of Commonwealth Bank; Klarna only resumed some hiring and kept the assistant (the article says "partly taken back"). Say "Both took part of it back" or "Both had to backtrack".
6. **p33 notes "about a tenth of its net income".** $278 m is pre-tax, net income is after tax ($2.7 bn); after tax the charge was $208 m (about 8%). Say "$278 million before tax, against $2.7 billion net income that quarter", or "close to a tenth".
7. **p28–30 notes, Bitkom "depend strongly on American technology".** Self-assessment; the base may be the firms that import digital goods. Say "about half … say they are strongly dependent on the US".
8. **p10 notes "In Germany it's mostly brick".** Brick in the strict sense is 30%; masonry together is two thirds. Say "mostly masonry, brick and block".
9. **p8–9 "nothing in between is published".** An absence claim. Say "I found nothing published in between".
10. **Title date: 12 December 2026 is a Saturday.** Confirm against the course schedule (no change if it's a block day).
